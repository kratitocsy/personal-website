01 Main project

Charge the car while it drives.

Wireless EV charging built into highway guardrails and median barriers, powered by solar panels along the road. Idea stage: tested by computer simulation, not yet built.

01
Research in progress

Cross-embodiment robot learning

Moving what one robot learns to a different robot type is a big bottleneck in robotics. My idea: classify robots the way Carl Linnaeus classified living species, grouping them by how well their parts (motors, joints, torque) transfer to one another, so a robot can learn from the best-matching donor type.

  • Building an SDK for robot failure logging: when an incident happens, record torque, motor power and similar data from first principles and send it to the cloud for simulation.
  • Early result: matching individual parts across randomised robot combinations has so far beaten transferring a whole robot to another whole robot. These are early results and may change as I test more.
