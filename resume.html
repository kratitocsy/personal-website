Maglion · idea, applying for a grant to test it

Founder

  • Wireless EV charging built into highway guardrails and median barriers, with charging pads for cities, powered by solar panels along the roadside.
  • Goal: smaller EV batteries and less charging time. Not yet built or measured; the first step is a low-cost bench test.
  • Why it matters: the battery is the biggest cost of an EV. If cars charge while driving, they could carry a battery up to about half the size. This is my estimate from simulations, not a measured result.
  • Ran computer simulations suggesting it can work. Dynamic wireless charging has been done by an Israel-based company, but set-up and maintenance cost is the problem; Maglion aims to cut it by 3× to 5× using guardrails and solar power (a target, not yet proven).
  • Applied to Emergent Ventures for a grant; awaiting a reply.

Cross-embodiment robot learning · research in progress

Research

  • Building an SDK for robot failure logging that lets data move from one robot type to another with maximum positive transfer.
  • Idea: classify robots the way Carl Linnaeus classified living species, grouping them by how much one robot's parts (motors, joints, torque) transfer positively to another, so a robot can learn from the best-matching donor type.
  • Early result: randomised combinations of parts from multiple robots, matched which parts give the most positive transfer, and stress-tested the combined result. So far this beats transferring a whole robot to another whole robot; early results that may change with more testing.
  • Status: still researching classification by maximum positive transfer, identifying failures, and 3D AI simulation for learning from other robots.
